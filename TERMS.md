## English

Users are responsible for reviewing source interpretation, missing values, topology and ratings with qualified personnel before using the JSON for engineering work. VeraGrid results are available only through the governed Web application after its validation and human-approval gates. The plugin is provided as-is without warranties, to the extent permitted by law.

Use must comply with applicable law, OpenAI policies, ChatGPT terms and the user's authorization to process supplied documents.
